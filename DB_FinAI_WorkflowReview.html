↑ When Fin AI Hands Over to Human Agents

IMMEDIATE — No follow-up questions

  • Illness, adverse reaction, ER/doctor visit, injury from product
  • Abnormal taste, smell, potency, contamination, foreign material
  • Damaged/leaking/tampered product or broken seal
  • Incorrect/missing/expired labeling or dosage info
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding, or minors mentioned re: product use
  • Dependency, overuse, increasing dosage, difficulty stopping
  • Chargeback or bank dispute threatened/initiated
  • Legal action, lawyers, lawsuits, product harm claims
  • Regulators, government bodies, official inquiries
  • Media or press asking about safety or incidents
  • Wrong item received or missing items
  • Package marked delivered but not received
  • Personal data request or deletion request
  • Kratom + medication interaction questions
  • Active withdrawal symptoms or health crisis
  • Wholesale, B2B, reseller, or partnership enquiries
  • Formal legal/regulatory communication
  • VIP, wholesale, or high-value account issues

AFTER INFO COLLECTION

  • Coupon/discount not working → after screenshot + code + email collected
  • Shipping address change → after new address confirmed
  • Subscription charged but not fulfilled → answer first, then handover
  • Active return/exchange/RMA requests → always handover
  • Shipping delay refund → after order # and dates collected
  • Customer repeats question or insists on different outcome
  • Customer explicitly requests a human agent
  • Query outside available knowledge
